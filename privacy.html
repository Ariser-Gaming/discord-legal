Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 8 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ariser Gaming ("Ariser Gaming", "we", "us", or "our") may collect, process, use, and retain information when you use our Discord applications and related services.

We aim to collect and process only information that is reasonably necessary for the operation and improvement of our applications.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the application and features you use, we may process information made available through Discord's API or provided directly through interaction with the application.

This may include:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord usernames and display names.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Discord channel IDs.
  • Discord role IDs.
  • Information contained in interactions with the application.
  • Configuration information required for the application to function.

The exact information processed depends on the specific Ariser Gaming application and the features enabled within it.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and operating application features.
  • Responding to commands and interactions.
  • Maintaining application configuration.
  • Monitoring and troubleshooting technical issues.
  • Preventing abuse or misuse of our services.
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of our applications.

4. Discord Information

Our applications operate through Discord. Discord may provide information to applications through its APIs depending on the permissions and functionality being used.

Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's Privacy Policy .

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by third-party infrastructure or service providers where necessary to operate the application, such as hosting providers, databases, logging systems, or services required for technical functionality.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide application functionality, maintain security, resolve technical issues, or satisfy legitimate operational requirements.

Where information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymised where reasonably practical.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by our applications against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Requests

If you believe that an Ariser Gaming application has stored information relating to you and you would like to request access, correction, or deletion, please contact the Ariser Gaming administration team through the official Ariser Gaming Discord community.

9. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from individuals in violation of applicable age requirements or platform rules.

Users should comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies when using our applications.

10. Third-Party Services

Our applications may depend on third-party services, including Discord, hosting providers, databases, and other infrastructure.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our applications, practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be available at this address.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the Ariser Gaming administration team through the official Ariser Gaming Discord community.
